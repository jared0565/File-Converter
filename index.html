      A few things to know

      Will my document look exactly the same?

      Document conversions focus on editable text and basic structure. Fonts, columns, tables, embedded images and exact page layout may change or be omitted. PDF-to-Word and PDF-to-Markdown extract selectable text. PDF-to-image exports preserve the rendered appearance of each page.

      Can I convert a scanned or password-protected PDF?

      Scanned PDFs can be exported as images, but text recognition (OCR) is not included. Text conversions require selectable text. Password-protected PDFs must be unlocked before conversion.

      What happens to my files?

      Your files are processed in this browser and are never uploaded. Unsaved files and results are held in memory until you remove them, choose another file or output format, or leave this page. Explicitly saved files remain in this browser profile until deleted or site data is cleared. They are not uploaded or synced across devices. Cloudflare serves the app and receives normal website requests, but no file contents. There are no analytics or third-party conversion services.

      Are there any conversion limits?

      Files can be up to 20 MB. Text is limited to one million characters, images to 24 megapixels, and PDFs to 200 pages for text or 30 pages for image export. CSV and JSON tables support up to 20,000 rows and 200 columns. JSON must be an array of objects. PDF generation supports Latin, Greek and Cyrillic text; unsupported characters produce an error rather than a broken file. Use a current browser for best results.
